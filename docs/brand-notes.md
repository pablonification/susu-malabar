## Type on their posters

No poster names its fonts. What they look like:

- **Menu poster header "MALABAR":** heavy, hand-cut, uneven capitals with a dark outline,
  white on dark green. Item names ("Strawberry Malabar") in a chalk-like handwriting, with
  chalk doodles around (arrows, clouds, sun, a banana).
- **Promo headlines ("PROMO SPESIAL", "RASA BARU!"):** fat rounded letters with a thick
  outline and a slight arc, like a sticker. Prices in a very heavy condensed grotesque ("10K").
  Small text in a plain bold geometric sans.
- **Calm posts ("Kami ada di mana?", "Outlet 1"):** a light serif, upright on the cover
  ("Kami ada di mana?") and italic on the slides ("Outlet 1"), with a plain sans for addresses. The strawberry photo set uses classic serif capitals
  ("SUSU MALABAR / STROBERI") and a widely spaced light sans.
- **The badge sign:** thin hand-drawn condensed capitals, "MALABAR".
- **Shop banners:** white monospace-like capitals on teal ("SUSU / MALABAR").

So there are two families: loud and bouncy (promos, menu) and quiet serif (stories,
locations). They are rarely mixed on the same post.

## How promos are laid out

Dark or photo ground. A bouncy outlined headline at the top, the price huge in the middle,
the old price crossed out with a red X ("~~13K~~"), the days small ("SELASA-RABU 16-17
Maret 2026"), a small red tag ("SERBU"), the round banana sticker at the top centre, tiny
corner labels ("TIBA-TIBA PROMO"), and a footer strip with the hashtag and a red pill
("BELI SEKARANG"). The "Rasa Baru" poster swaps the dark ground for a sky photo and real
hands holding the cups.

## Photo style

Phone photos, daylight or overcast, often a little dark. Shot from above or high: the cup
on a table, the cup in a hand, the blender from overhead ("Dilihat dari atas, diracik dengan
tenang."). Real places around the cup: a bike, a scooter, the street, the zinc counter. Lots
of behind-the-counter video: pouring milk, the red blender lid, corndogs in the fryer basket,
staff bagging stacks of white cups at night under bright tube lights. Staff in plain dark
t-shirts. Customers are shown from behind, or blurred.

Lines that repeat: "Kemurnian dari Jatinangor" (Linktree, TikTok bio, the banner at the
MST shop), "MILKSHAKE WITH FRESH TASTE" (menu poster, banner), "real milk.real fruit"
(sticker), #kesegarandarijatinangor, #kemurnianjatinangor. Menu names follow
"<flavour> Malabar": "Strawberry Malabar", "Mango Malabar", "Chocolate Malabar",
"Avocado Malabar", "Banana Malabar", "Melon Malabar", "Kurma Malabar", "Taro Malabar".
Shops are named by what they face: "depan Griya", "depan MST", "depan Ikopin". The admin
signs as "minbar". Customers are "teman-teman", "warga nangor".

Not seen in any current post: "Corndog Dong", "Berduaan maniiss", "Traktir Drivermu Yuk"
(they may be GoFood item names; GoFood did not load).

## How they write the name

"Susu Malabar" on profiles; "susu malabar" in lowercase on poster footers and the sticker;
"SUSU MALABAR" on banners; "MALABAR" alone on the badge, the painted wall and the menu
header; "Milkbar Susu Malabar" on the MST banner; "malabar" as the short name in captions
("dari malabar", "malabar regal"); once "Susumalabar" as one word.

## Recurring motifs

The shop-front badge (striped awning, double doors, chalkboard). Tall white cups with the
black badge. Fruit drawn or painted (on the wall, on the stickers). Hands holding a cup.
The dark green field. Teal walls and stools. Chalk doodles on the menu.

## Use on the site:

- Dark poster green `#184A44` as the main dark. The awning's turquoise for the shops' band,
  a step deeper than in the photo (`#14B3AB`) so black type reads on it and the awning in the
  photo still stands out. One pink, as an accent: the petal coral `#F39E8D` of the
  Instagram feed, for strawberry and for what is in the order. The hot TikTok badge pink
  `#F784C5` stays on the tab icon only: in big blocks it is not how the feed looks.
- Type: the heavy sans for everything the site says. The upright serif of "Kami ada di
  mana?" only for the shop's own lines and review quotes. No italics.
- A little of the promo posters, where it has a job: the best seller's block (a small black
  tag, the name huge), the order bar (the sum as big as a poster's price), and the form's
  total above the send button (a small line, the sum huge under it: the form's one loud line).
- The real badge in one colour (`logo-badge.svg`, PNG when it is small), black on white like
  the cups.
- Real photos: strawberry milk first, each shop's front a small square inside its own row.
  No photo of a person at work: they are soft video stills, and nobody knows who is in them.
- Their framing: from above, a hand and a cup, the real street around it.
- Their words: "Kemurnian dari Jatinangor", "<flavour> Malabar" names, "kamu/kalian".
- Shops by landmark, the way they say it: "depan Griya", "depan MST", "depan Ikopin".
- A big price with the old one crossed out, only when there is a real promo.

## Avoid:

- Many typefaces in one place (serif italic, bubbly outline, condensed, chalk handwriting).
  Pick one loud face for headings at most.
- Text and stickers over photos, and blocky blurred faces.
- The stock cut-outs on the event menu (pasted fruit and milk bottles).
- The sky-gradient background of "Rasa Baru" (the site has no gradients).
- Cows, the milk supplier and anything farm-like (kept off the site on purpose).
- Dark, unedited night shots used as they are.
- Emoji as decoration, ALL CAPS shouting, and placeholder text like "⏰ ............".
